Topic E: SME Finance Copilot. One story, one synthetic company, every rubric line shown on screen.
Company: **Synthetic Trading Co.**, a Malaysian home-goods importer (synthetic data, labelled on screen).

Recording notes: 1440×900 browser, light theme, English UI (switch to 中文 once in scene 7). Record each scene separately; trim pauses. Narration is the text in quotes; aim for about 130 words a minute.

## Before recording

- Use the demo build with synthetic data; keep the "Demo data" notices visible — they are part of the honesty story.
- Pre-create the clerk and owner sessions in two browser profiles so scene 5 has no sign-in delay.
- Rehearse with a timer; cut narration rather than speeding up.
- Export as MP4 (H.264). Provide a mirror reachable from mainland China, not YouTube.

## Claims to avoid on screen

- No real bank names as partners, no "approved loan", no certifications we do not hold.
- Say "matches", "illustrative terms" and "estimated hours saved", exactly as the UI does.
